# studio.hdr

The 3D phone's studio environment (owner decision 15). Used by `../hdri.ts` and `../environment.ts` as the
reflection environment (PMREM), inlined into the lazily loaded engine chunk; flat projects never load it.

- Asset: "Studio Small 09" by Sergej Majboroda, Poly Haven
- Page: https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_09
- File: https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/HDRIs/hdr/2k/studio_small_09_2k.hdr (2048 x 1024, Radiance RGBE, RLE)
- SHA-256: 36724313c0fc66dab126ff90f081b85a0b1ef47be65eda1417bd20b033f0573f (the downloaded file, unchanged)
- Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), https://polyhaven.com/license: free for any use, commercial
  included, redistribution in products allowed, no attribution required (credited here anyway).
- Downloaded and verified: 2026-10-25.

Why this one: three CC0 Poly Haven studios were rendered on the 17 Pro turntable (Deep Blue, Silver, Cosmic
Orange; turns 0-180) at four panorama turns each: studio_small_09, photo_studio_loft_hall, studio_kontrast_04.
studio_small_09 (a white cyclorama on one side, dark room and two gridded octaboxes on the other) gave the
metal the strongest structured reflections; studio_kontrast_04 is low contrast (the back stayed one tone) and
photo_studio_loft_hall is a warm room with windows (lens covers flooded white, finishes tinted brown).
